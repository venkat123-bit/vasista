1. Where we deliver

We deliver to the locations we can serve from our kitchen and partner couriers. Enter your full address and pincode at checkout. If we cannot deliver to your area, we will tell you on WhatsApp or by phone before dispatch, and you can cancel for a full refund.

2. Processing time

Our snacks are packed close to your delivery date and not stockpiled. Orders are normally packed and dispatched within 1–3 working days of confirmation.

3. Delivery time

After dispatch, delivery usually takes 1–5 working days depending on your location. Delays can happen because of weather, festivals or courier issues, and we will keep you informed on WhatsApp or by phone.

4. Delivery charges

Any delivery charge is confirmed to you before you pay. If none is mentioned, no delivery charge applies to your order.

5. Receiving your order

  • Please keep your phone reachable on the number you gave us.
  • Check the package when you receive it. If it is damaged or tampered with, take a photo and contact us within 24 hours (see our Refund & Cancellation Policy).
  • If delivery fails because the address or phone number is wrong or nobody is available, we may contact you to arrange redelivery, which can involve an extra charge.
